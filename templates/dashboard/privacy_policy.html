FitNova SaaS Privacy & Biometric Data Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At {{ current_org.name|default:"FitNova Elite Fitness" }}, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal, physiological, and biometric data processed within our enterprise fitness management platform.

2. Biometric Data Collection & Facial Vectors

Our Smart Biometric Turnstile system processes 128-point facial geometric landmarks strictly for identity verification, physical access control, and attendance logging.

  • No Raw Photos Stored: Camera feeds are processed into mathematical facial landmark vectors (128-dimension floating-point arrays). Raw image streams are discarded immediately following vector extraction.
  • Cryptographic Encryption: Stored facial profiles are hashed and encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Voluntary Enrollment: Members have full discretion to opt out of biometric access in favor of digital QR code passes.
3. Information We Collect

We collect personal identification information including full name, email address, mobile number, emergency contacts, subscription details, and training schedules.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

FitNova does not sell, license, or monetize member data to third-party advertisers. Information is only utilized to provide fitness facility services, generate tax-compliant invoices, and notify you of gym operations.

5. Data Retention & Right to Erasure

Members can request deletion of their facial vectors and profile information upon subscription termination by contacting our Data Protection Officer at {{ current_org.email|default:'privacy@fitnova.com' }}.
